## Is it a moderator?

No. The contract cuts the opening in code, validators label each question, and the verdict follows from the labels. Nobody decides whether the proposal is good.

## Who sets the questions?

The DAO, on a card. They are public.

## What if I disagree with a label?

The window, the questions and the prompt are all on the proposal page. The card owner can waive the rule for one proposal, and the waiver is public.

## Why was my ask MISSING when it is in the title?

The title counts toward the window, but the answer has to be specific. A title that names the work without an amount does not answer "how much it costs". The [evaluation](/docs/more/evaluation) publishes the cases where the validators and the spec disagree.

## Can I pad the top?

Padding makes the real text longer, so the ask falls below the line. Each answer must be specific, and voters still read the page.

## Which network is it on?

GenLayer Studio Next, chain 61997. See [Addresses](/docs/reference/addresses).

## Why did my transaction say ACCEPTED but nothing happened?

Validators can agree that a refusal is the correct result. Success is an accepted status **and** a leader execution that returned. The site shows the contract's own sentence when it refuses; see [Errors](/docs/reference/errors).
